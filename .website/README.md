Unicorns never stop firing rainbow beams from their eyes. Spin the plates to bend the light around the board and hit every cake with **exactly** the right color.

Beams pass *through* cakes, so one beam can feed several — and where two beams cross, their colors add up: red + green = yellow, all three = rainbow. Donuts let only their own color through, magic stars cycle red → green → blue, and choco blocks just eat the beam.

Clear the board and the unicorns trot over and eat everything, chomp by chomp.

**60 levels, zero level data in the zip.** Every level is generated by code and built *backwards* from a known solution, so it is always solvable. "Par" is the true minimum move count, found by exhaustive search — match it for ★★★. Hints cap you at ★★.

- Mouse, touch and keyboard — arrows + space, **Z** undo, **R** reset, **H** hint, **M** music
- Tiny WebAudio gallop tune with clip-clop percussion
- No libraries, no external assets

Made by Yellow Jasmin — pixel art and music drawn in-house.
